
# 📦 InventoryTracker PRO

**Private Asset Intelligence & Local Inventory Management.**

InventoryTracker PRO is a high-end, local-first enterprise utility built for business owners who need to manage physical assets and warehouse value with 100% data privacy. No monthly fees. No cloud risks.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To begin managing your assets, follow these three operational steps:

1. **Populate Your SKUs:** Navigate to the **"Active SKUs"** tab. Click **"+ Add SKU"** to enter your products, current stock levels, and unit prices.
2. **Configure Risk Alerts:** Set a **"Minimum Alert Level"** for each item. When stock drops below this point, the system triggers a **"REORDER REQ"** alert on your dashboard to prevent lost revenue.
3. **Monitor Real-Time Equity:** The **"Dashboard"** instantly calculates the total cash value of your physical inventory. This eliminates hours of manual bookkeeping for insurance and tax reporting.

## 🔒 The Privacy Moat
