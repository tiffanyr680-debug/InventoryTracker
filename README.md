# 📦 StockCommand PRO

**Private Asset Intelligence & Inventory Management for SMBs.**

StockCommand PRO is a local-first enterprise tool designed for business owners who need to track physical assets, monitor warehouse value, and prevent stock-outs without the security risks of cloud-based software.

## 🚀 How to Use the App

Follow these three steps to gain full control over your inventory:

1. **Initialize Your Warehouse:** Open the app and go to the **"Active SKUs"** tab. Click **"+ Add SKU"** to enter your products, their current quantities, and their individual unit prices.
2. **Set Logic Alerts:** For every item, define a **"Minimum Alert Level."** When your stock drops below this number, the dashboard will automatically trigger a red alert to prioritize your reordering.
3. **Monitor Total Equity:** The **"Dashboard"** tab provides a real-time dollar value calculation of your entire physical inventory. Use this data for accounting audits, insurance reporting, and tax season preparation.

## 🔒 Security & Performance

- **100% Local-First:** All inventory data is stored within your browser's local memory. We never transmit your sensitive business records to an external server.
- **Zero Ongoing Costs:** Unlike per-seat SaaS tools, StockCommand PRO is a one-time install. No monthly subscriptions. No API dependencies.
- **High Performance:** Built with Tailwind CSS and Alpine.js for an instant, lag-free user experience.

## 💰 The ROI

- **Prevent Lost Sales:** Avoid "Sold Out" scenarios with predictive stock alerts.
- **Cash Flow Optimization:** Identify which products are overstocked and tied up in capital.
- **Automated P&L:** Instantly see the value of your assets for your profit and loss statements.
