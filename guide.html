StockCommand PRO Quick Start

1. Add Your SKUs

Click "Add New SKU" to enter your products. Enter the current quantity and the "Min Alert" level (the level where you want the app to turn red).

2. Monitor Assets

Your Dashboard will automatically calculate the total dollar value of your entire warehouse based on your SKU data.

3. Restock Fast

When an item turns red in the "Restock Priority" list, it's time to reorder. This ensures you never miss a sale due to low stock.
